Where It Began · Kalahandi to Bengaluru

I come from Kalahandi, Odisha, where hills and paddy fields shaped my earliest understanding of the world. My journey from there to Bengaluru, and into the study of climate change and sustainability, has been anything but straightforward.

🧭

The Journey & The Turning Point

After my bachelor’s degree, my priority was to earn a living and support my family. I explored different paths, from flight steward training to bookkeeping at a car showroom. Each experience taught me something, but the desire to learn kept drawing me back. Through learning spaces and conversations with young people from across India, I began to see education as a way to understand both my own circumstances and the wider forces shaping people’s lives.

🎓

The Direction · Climate Change & Sustainability

That curiosity led me to pursue a master’s in Climate Change and Sustainability. It gave a new direction to questions rooted in where I grew up: how people depend on the environment, how opportunities are distributed, and what meaningful development should look like.

💼

Looking Ahead · Corporate Sustainability & Climate Finance

Today, I am working towards a career in corporate sustainability, with interests in ESG, climate finance, carbon markets, and nature-based solutions. I want to understand how businesses and financial decisions can support climate action—and help turn commitments into measurable outcomes for people and nature.

My Guiding Principle

“My path has changed over time, but I carry its lessons with me: stay curious, be willing to begin again, and keep the people affected by a decision at the centre of the work.”
